# GLACIAL FORENSICS — Multi-Temporal Proglacial Lake Expansion: Drang-Drung Glacier (2017–2024)

## 1. Executive Findings
Drang-Drung Glacier—one of the largest glaciers in the Zanskar Range (length ~23 km)—terminates into an expanding proglacial lake impounded by massive composite moraines near Pensi La Pass. Analysis of 4 multi-year Sentinel-1 SAR benchmarks confirms continuous proglacial water expansion driven by calving retreat of the glacier tongue.

## 4. Expansion Trajectory & Calving Dynamics
- **Growth Characteristics:** The proglacial water body has expanded steadily from 2017 to 2024, displaying an annualized surface growth rate of approximately +2 to +4 ha/yr.
- **Terminus Calving Recession:** The calving contact face has migrated steadily up-valley (southwestward), with the lake occupying the vacated subglacial overdeepening.
- **Ice-Contact Margin:** High radar backscatter contrast between low-return specular open water (<-16 dB) and rough, debris-mantled glacier ice (-6 to -10 dB) allows precise tracking of the calving front position.
