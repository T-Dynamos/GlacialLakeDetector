# GLACIAL FORENSICS — DEM & Topographic Validation: Kyagar Glacier & Shaksgam Gorge

## 1. Digital Elevation Model Verification
Terrain metrics were derived from Copernicus GLO-30 DSM tile `Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N35_00_E077_00_DEM.tif` (30m spatial resolution), transformed to UTM Zone 43N (EPSG:32643) with valid alpine relief masking.

## 2. Terrain Metrics
- **Analysis Window:** 10 km x 10 km (100 km²) encompassing the Kyagar Glacier snout and the Shaksgam River impoundment zone.
- **Elevation Range:** **4,680 m to 6,450 m a.s.l.** (Rugged Karakoram alpine relief; total relief: 1,770 m).
- **Lake Surface Elevation at Peak:** **4,785 m a.s.l.**
- **Surging Ice-Dam Thickness:** Ice tongue dam crest reaches **4,840 m to 4,870 m a.s.l.** (effective ice dam height: **55 m to 85 m**).
- **Valley Bottleneck Width:** The Shaksgam River valley narrows to approximately **1,100 m to 1,300 m** where the Kyagar Glacier tongue collides with the opposing bedrock cliff.
- **Gorge Wall Slopes:** Valley walls opposing the glacier snout exhibit extreme declivities exceeding **45° to 65°**.

## 3. Hydrologic Trajectory & Downstream Basin
The impounded lake sits along the upper Shaksgam River channel. When the ice dam breaches, outburst floodwaters discharge northwestward through the deeply entrenched Shaksgam River gorge into the Yarkant River basin, traversing the Tarim Basin in Xinjiang, China.
