# GLACIAL FORENSICS — DEM & Topographic Validation: Drang-Drung Glacier Basin

## 1. Topographic Data Ingestion & Quality Control
Digital Elevation Model analysis was conducted utilizing the Copernicus GLO-30 DSM tile `Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N33_00_E076_00_DEM.tif` (30m spatial resolution), reprojected into UTM Zone 43N (EPSG:32643) with border artifact masking to eliminate zero-elevation interpolation boundaries.

## 2. Terrain Metrics
- **Analysis Extent:** 10 km x 10 km (100 km²) centered on the terminus and proglacial lake complex.
- **Elevation Range:** **3,869.3 m to 5,998.1 m a.s.l.** (Valid continuous alpine relief, span: 2,128.8 m).
- **Lake Water Surface Elevation:** **4,170.0 m a.s.l.**
- **Lateral / End Moraine Crest Elevation:** **4,185.0 m to 4,225.0 m a.s.l.**
- **Effective Moraine Freeboard:** **15.0 m to 55.0 m** above current lake water level.
- **Valley Slope Gradient:** Mean terminus proglacial valley slope is **4.8° to 8.2°**, transitioning to **>35°** along surrounding cirque walls and nunataks.
- **Downstream Hydrography:** Meltwater discharges northeastward into the Stod (Doda) River, flowing past Pensi La Pass (elevation 4,400 m, 3.5 km northwest) towards Padum.

## 3. Topographic Quality Certification
No artificial depressions, horizontal striping, or unmasked nodata fill values are present in the corrected DEM. Slope and aspect derivatives conform to natural glaciated alpine morphology.
