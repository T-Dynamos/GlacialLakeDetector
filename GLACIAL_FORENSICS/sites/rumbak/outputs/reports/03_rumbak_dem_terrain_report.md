# GLACIAL FORENSICS — Priority 1: Rumbak Topography & Flow Hydraulic Report

## 1. Topographic Setting
The Copernicus GLO-30 DSM was reprojected and clipped to UTM Zone 43N (`EPSG:32643`) across the $90\text{ km}^2$ analysis domain.

- **Elevation Range:** $3,814.8\text{ m}$ to $5,588.6\text{ m a.s.l.}$
- **Mean Elevation:** $4,852.1\text{ m}$
- **Lake Cirque Elevation:** $5,268\text{ m a.s.l.}$
- **Rumbak Village Elevation:** $3,870\text{ m a.s.l.}$
- **Vertical Descent:** **$1,398\text{ m}$ vertical drop** over a **$7.0\text{ km}$ drainage channel**.

## 2. Channel Hydraulic Gradient
$$\text{Average Slope Gradient} = \frac{1398\text{ m}}{7000\text{ m}} = 0.200 \quad (11.31^\circ)$$

This steep average hydraulic gradient explains the extreme velocity and bulk sediment carrying capacity of the August 2021 debris flow. Boulders exceeding $2\text{ m}$ in diameter were mobilized from the moraine dam breach and transported down into the Rumbak valley plain.
