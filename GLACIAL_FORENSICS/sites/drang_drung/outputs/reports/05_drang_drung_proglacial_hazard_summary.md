# GLACIAL FORENSICS — GLOF Susceptibility & Proglacial Hazard Assessment: Drang-Drung Glacier

## 1. Hazard Characterization
- **Lake Type:** Proglacial Moraine-Dammed Lake with Active Calving Ice-Contact Front.
- **Impoundment Geometry:** Broad composite terminal moraine complex with substantial lateral width (>250 m) and freeboard exceeding 25 m.
- **Outflow Regime:** Well-defined natural bedrock/boulder spillway draining continuously into the upper Stod (Doda) River gorge.

## 2. Trigger Mechanism & Breach Vulnerability
1. **Ice Avalanches & Calving Displacement Waves:** The primary threat stems from hanging ice/rock avalanches from steep flanking cirques (slopes >45°) or catastrophic calving of the terminus cliff, generating seiche waves capable of overtopping the moraine.
2. **Moraine Piping / Degradation:** Buried ice cores within the terminal moraine are vulnerable to thermokarst subsidence as permafrost degrades under regional warming.
3. **Downstream Infrastructure Exposure:** The Kargil-Zanskar highway (NH-301) transits through Pensi La Pass (~3.5 km away) and descends into the Stod Valley, crossing multiple bridges and culverts directly vulnerable to outburst floods.

## 4. Monitoring Recommendations
Bi-weekly Sentinel-1 SAR amplitude and coherence tracking during June–October ablation seasons to detect accelerated calving or moraine crest subsidence prior to potential overtopping.
